# ÉclaireSkin SEO opportunity map

Date: 15 August 2026

## Measurement note

Public keyword-volume sources disagree substantially because they use different countries, match types, databases and query forms. The figures found during this research are global or US-oriented, not verified New Zealand monthly volumes. They are useful as directional demand evidence only.

Before removing `noindex`, validate the final target terms in Google Keyword Planner, Semrush or Ahrefs with New Zealand selected as the market. Record monthly volume, keyword difficulty, SERP intent and the date checked in this file.

## Demand signals

- Third-party July 2026 data places `skincare` and `skincare routine` among the largest broad category terms. These are high demand and very competitive.
- A 2025 Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology study using Google Trends found retinol had the strongest interest among the cosmeceuticals studied, followed by hyaluronic acid, salicylic acid, glycolic acid and vitamin C.
- Ingredient-volume datasets consistently show material interest in retinol, hyaluronic acid, niacinamide, vitamin C, salicylic acid and peptides, although their exact estimates vary widely.
- Google's own guidance says health-adjacent content receives stronger trust scrutiny. Clear authorship, sourcing, review status and original value therefore matter more than producing a large number of pages.
